# Election 

## Timing
After the Season Winner has been decided the Final Lap page is updated with the new winner.

On Thursday, The Final Lap voting becomes visible and usable for logged in users

On Sunday at 1 PM EST, Voting is closed and the results for each vote is announced.

This screen stays until Tuesday, when the screen is locked up, saying "Next Season Voting will be revealed Thursday"

### Sponsor Help
"Lend your support or disgust to our Eternal Racers"

Each election offers five randomly selected actions from the pool below. The same five options remain available to everyone throughout that election.

Users buy entries for 10 Coin each. Each action is its own raffle: when voting closes, one purchased entry is randomly selected for each action that has entries. Multiple entries increase the chance of that driver selection winning within its action. An action with no entries produces no change, so there are at most five outcomes.

Feel free to use a modal to help with selection.


- Car Swap
    - Swap cars between two Drivers (select both)

- Haunting
    - A driver gains the Haunted Modifer (select Driver)

- Soul Swap
    - Two Drivers swap Reflex, Pride, and Focus (Select Drivers)

- White Coffee
    - Driver becomes 'Fritez' Modifer (Select Driver)

- Eye Exam
    - Driver gains 'Beautiful Vision' Modifer

- Tune Down 
    - Driver's Car gains 'Unheard Frequency' Modifer

- Reflective Paint
    - Driver's Car gains 'Shiny' Modifer

- Double Agent
    - Driver swaps Teams with a random other driver (Select one Driver)

- Golden Child
    - Driver's Tema Mates -10 Luck, Driver gets +30 Luck (Select one Driver)
