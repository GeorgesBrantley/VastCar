# Items
Items are objects that can be bought by Fans
Items can be Active (In your inventory) or Inactive (in your Stash)
They are dragged from one to the other, and are swapped out (save button) 
Players can update their Inventory at any point

Items are bought at the Race Tower. Items can stack 10 times per spot.. If you buy the item for the 11th time, it will take another inventory/stash space.

Items are bought with Coin. Items can be sold for 60% value back to the Tower, at the Tower.

You cannot buy a new item if your inventory is full... or you are buying a new item and there is no room. (if I have 3 stacks of 10 binoculars active, and 4 stacks of 10 beer in my stash, along with another stack of 3 beer, I can only buy beer (to add to the 3 stack, and not any other item))

## Items List
### Idle Items
Do not label these idle items in game. These pop based on race events

#### Binoculars
Cost: 100 Coin
Effect: Everytime your Favorite Racer wins gain 10 Coin

#### Beer
Cost: 20 Coin
Effect: Everytime your Favorite Racer gets bottom 3, gain 5 Coin

#### Whistle
Cost: 25 Coin
Effect: Everytime your Favorite Racer gets top 3, gain 5 Coin

#### Camera
Cost: 30 Coin
Effect: Everytime there is a crash, gain 10 coin

#### Old Scroll
Cost: 10 Coin
Effect: Everytime someone wins for the first time that season, gain 10 Coin

#### Watch
Cost: 10 Coin
Effect: Gain 50 coins when any race takes over 8 minutes.

#### Team Flag
Cost: 50 Coin
Effect: Gain 20 coins When a Driver on a team you sponsor wins

#### Grandfather Clock
Cost: 10 Coin
Effect: Gain 50 coins when the winning time for a race is under 7:30.



### Betting Items

#### Vegas Shark
Cost: 50 Coin
Effect: Each Vegas Shark decreases the winning penalty for bets by 2 (minimum 0), so if you have 10 vegas sharks and you place a bet on a car that has won 3 times, the total cost is 5+MAX[12-(3*2),0]=11 instead of 5+12=17. 

#### Sunglasses
Cost: 50 Coin
Effect: When you bet on a racer with 0 wins, if they win you get 500 bonus Coin.

#### Gun
Cost: 100 Coin
Effect: Increase the amount you can bet per Racer by 2 bets

#### Lucky ticket
Cost: 1 Coin
Effect: If you only bet on one driver in a race, Payout is +300

#### Insurance
Cost: 70 Coin
Effect: If a Car you bet on expereinces a crash, gain +100
